01

Name Each Part For Its Job

The semantic layer. A part stops being navy-900 and becomes “the foreground”, or “the band”. Re-theming edits this layer and nothing else.

Semantic tokenPartJob
--color-pageblue-50The page ground. The light blue everything sits on
--color-bgwhiteThe plates content sits on
--color-bandnavy-900The full-bleed navy band surface
--color-fgnavy-900Body and heading text on light
--color-fg-mutednavy-400Secondary text on light
--color-interactiveblue-600Links, focus, available slots — on light
--color-bordergrey-200Hairlines on light
--color-focusblue-600Focus ring — restyled, never removed
--color-gradient-endblue-100Terminal stop of the two gradients in the system

Why --color-fg-muted is not a grey. The page ground is blue-50, and grey-500 computes to 4.44:1 there — under AA. A hue-tinted mute clears both grounds: 6.38:1 on blue, 6.94:1 on white. On a coloured surface, tint the secondary text from that hue rather than reaching for grey.

Three hops, one direction. --navy-900 → --color-accent-strong → --button-primary-bg. A component that reaches straight to var(--navy-900) looks identical today and breaks the moment the theme moves — silently, which is why the indirection is worth its extra token. Developers copy code, not prose, so this stylesheet is the reference the landing page inherits.

02

On A Navy Band

Every colour decision has two answers. Six semantic rows exist only for this surface, and skipping them is the most likely way this redesign ships an accessibility failure — because the failure is invisible in a light-only screenshot.

On bandPartRatio on navy-900Job
--color-fg-on-bandblue-5015.69:1Body and heading text
--color-fg-muted-on-bandnavy-2007.91:1Secondary text, captions, metadata
--color-interactive-on-bandblue-4006.72:1Accent word, rule numerals, links
--color-focus-on-bandblue-4006.72:1Focus ring
--color-border-on-bandnavy-7001.31:1Hairlines — structure, not state
on-band primary buttonblue-600 fill5.17:1 white on itA navy button on a navy band is invisible

These tokens exist because the redesign concept got this wrong, measurably. In the prototype the Ketentuan band used blue-600 for its accent word and its rule numerals, and navy-400 for the note beneath the list. On navy-900 those compute 3.30:1 and 2.46:1. The first clears AA only as large text; the second fails outright at any size. That band is where the ten rules a visitor is agreeing to are read, so it is the worst place on the page to be a hundredth of a ratio short.

The constraint runs both ways. blue-400 on white computes 2.54:1 and must never appear on a light surface; navy-200 is lighter still. A component that renders in both a light and a navy section reads its colour from the semantic layer, never from a primitive — that is exactly what these rows are for.

The navy-700 hairline is 1.31:1 and that is correct. It is structure, not state. Any boundary on a navy band that carries a state must clear 3:1, which is why no interactive control lives inside a reading band — and why nothing here takes a shadow. A shadow on a dark surface is either invisible or a glow, and glow is the one thing the client ruled out by name.

The rows above are the treatment, not just the tokens. On the landing page the ten-rule Ketentuan renders exactly like the numbered rows in the section above this one — full width, a leaning outlined ordinal each, navy-700 hairlines. That text is verbatim client content and lives in one guarded place, docs/PRD.md compared character-for-character against the shipped copy by check:docs. It is deliberately not duplicated onto this page: an unguarded fourth copy of client-owned text is the exact drift this project has already paid for three times.

03

The Axis, The Outline

Two tokens carry the whole direction, and every leaning element reads from them rather than restating a number. A second skew value anywhere in the system is a defect, not a variation.

TokenValueWhat uses it
--skew-8degSection numerals, rule numerals, the hero eyebrow rule, the button wipe, the marquee band
--diag168degGradient axis — the hero shader fallback, the map placeholder

-8deg rather than the mark's own 20°. At 20° an uppercase display-face numeral at 144px overhangs its own column by more than 40px and collides with the heading beside it at every width below 900px. The axis is the mark's direction, sampled and reduced until it survives 375px. It is also the cheapest identity available: transform: skewX() is compositor-only and costs nothing per frame.

The outline

Outlined type is the system's boldest typographic device and the reason it needs no second colour, no second typeface, and no highlight box. On the landing page it appears in exactly four places. Nothing outlined is ever smaller than 24px — below that the stroke eats the counters and the glyph stops being a letter.

Pilih Jam.
Kirim.
Main.

the hero headline · 48px here · 152px at 1440 · `KIRIM.` outlined 2px blue-600

Its failure mode is invisibility, not ugliness, and it fails in three ways a screenshot never shows. So the transparency lives inside @supports (-webkit-text-stroke: 1px currentColor) with the word filled solid as the fallback; @media (forced-colors: active) drops the transparency and fills with CanvasText, because Windows High Contrast Mode does not honour a text stroke; and nothing is outlined below 24px. Outlined text stays in the DOM as text, is never an image, and is never the only place a word appears.

The gradient axis

linear-gradient(168deg, blue-50 → white 45% → blue-100)

The same axis at 168deg. It is the hero shader's static fallback — used when WebGL2 is unavailable, when the visitor asked for reduced motion, or when saveData is set — and the ground of the map placeholder. That fallback is why blue-100 exists as a token, and why the hero looks deliberate rather than broken on the device most likely to hit it.

The band that leans

9 SLOT / HARI///06.00 — 24.00/// WITA///BOOKING VIA WHATSAPP/// TANPA AKUN///14 HARI KE DEPAN

the hero marquee · band skewed -1.2deg · type stays horizontal · rendered still here

-1.2deg is not a second axis value. It is the same lean read at band scale: a full-width band skewed -8deg would drop its corner more than 100px below the fold at 1280px. The band's edge leans; the content on it does not, so the type stays horizontal and legible. There is exactly one marquee in the system, it carries facts rather than slogans, it is aria-hidden — so every fact in it appears somewhere reachable — and in production it stops when it leaves the viewport, under reduced motion, and under saveData. The clock is WITA, never WIB and never a Jakarta-derived time: the field is in Lombok and the entire date layer pins Asia/Makassar.

Body copy in Plus Jakarta Sans. Saira at wdth 125 is wide and geometric, so it takes tighter leading than the body face or it reads as loose — 0.95 for display, 1.05 for section headings, 1.25 for sub-headings, 1.6 for body. Never apply body leading to the display face.

--text-body · Plus Jakarta Sans 400 · 16→18px · 1.6 · measure capped at 60–68ch

The 3rem floor is a measurement, not a taste. At a 375px viewport clamp(3rem, 1rem + 11vw, 9.5rem) resolves to 57.25px — not the 48px floor, which is only reached below about 291px — and “Pilih Jam.” renders 323px wide there against a 343px content box. That is the binding number in the whole type system: it is why the hero headline's longest word is five characters, and why a copy change there is a layout change. Measured in a browser with the display face loaded, at the size the clamp actually produces. Re-measured on the 2026-08-12 typeface change: Saira ExtraExpanded renders it at 323.0px, within 0.4px of the Orbitron it replaced — which is why that face was chosen over Archivo Expanded, whose 384.6px would have broken this number outright.

Uppercase is display only. display, numeral, h2 and the eyebrow are uppercase; body, sm and xs never are. Uppercase Indonesian body text is measurably slower to read, and the Ketentuan is the longest reading on the page.

Two faces, and this document no longer has a third. It used to set its own chrome in Archivo so the specimens read as “the product's faces”. The redesign made this page the prototype, so that third face became the third face the system forbids; it is gone. Both remaining faces are a stated client commitment. In production they load through next/font/google, self-hosted with zero layout shift — never a CDN <link> like the one at the top of this file.

05

The Slot Cell

One column on a phone. Two from 640px. Three from 1180px. The constraint is one number: “Menunggu Konfirmasi” is 20 characters and renders 143.3px wide at the cell's 14px Plus Jakarta Sans — measured with the font loaded, not estimated. At 375px a 3-column grid gives about 106px per cell, roughly 71px of it inside the padding, so the label cannot fit at all; the phone keeps a single full-width column, where 143.3px sits inside a 343px row with room to spare. Click an available slot, then click it again to deselect.

Terisi

The legend carries the three live states only. elapsed is explained by its own collapsed group's label, and a fourth row would imply elapsed slots are something a visitor might act on.

The breakpoints moved down from 768/1024 because the grid no longer measures the viewport. It measures the panel, which is 1.1fr of a 1280px two-column layout above 980px and full width below it. At a 640px viewport the panel is roughly 570px, so two columns give about 280px each and the 20-character label fits with room to spare. Three columns wait until 1180px, where each cell still clears 143.3px comfortably. The state label sits under the time at every width, which is what keeps all 20 characters at any column count.

Selection lives in aria-pressed, not in a class. A class-only selection looks correct and is silent to a screen reader, and this is the pattern the landing page copies into the product's core interaction. Unavailable cells stay visible and focusable via aria-disabled, never native disabled — a user needs to see that 18.00 is taken.

The selected cell's state label is full white, not 85% white. This is a deliberate departure from DESIGN.md's Slot Cell section, recomputed rather than copied: white at 85% over blue-600 composites to rgb(222,232,252) and measures 4.20:1 against the fill, below the 4.5:1 AA bar for 14px text. Full white is 5.17:1. The label separates by size and face, not by transparency — this page already took a P0 for the identical mistake on the pill's day label.

Elapsed hours are not booked hours. GET /api/availability returns booked for today's past slots, but the client already knows the time and the canonical starts, so it derives “elapsed” itself — no API change, contract stays firm. It is the neutral family (grey-200 / navy-400, 5.61:1) against the danger one, plus the label — “Sudah lewat” against “Terisi” — which is what satisfies WCAG 1.4.1, since colour is never the only means. Both are unavailable, but only one of them is somebody else's booking and the visitor is entitled to see which.

The free-run badge is the one place the design serves the client's own metric. The client measures this product by whether dead hours get booked, and a grid that renders 07.00 and 20.00 identically is neutral about that. “Bisa main {n} jam berturut-turut” is derived from the nine statuses already returned — no new endpoint, no price, no claim that cannot be computed. Minimum three consecutive available slots, one badge per date on the run's first cell only, and pending, booked and elapsed all break a run. It never says “take this dead hour”; it answers a question the organiser genuinely has, which is can we play longer?

Hover takes the glow, and the glow is blue on purpose. The Tinted-Shadow Rule is written against neutral black, which reads as dirt on a blue-white page. glow-interactive is the same event as the Signal Blue border it sits under, not a lighting effect — the palette behaving consistently. It never appears on a disabled cell, and it never appears inside a navy band.

06

Date Pills

The only fully round shape in the system, and its roundness is functional: the row reads as horizontally scrollable without an arrow, a fade, or a hint label. overscroll-behavior-x: contain stops a swipe bouncing the page. Tab through to reach the unavailable one.

The One-Round-Shape Rule. Nothing else is fully round. The moment a second element takes the pill radius, the date row stops meaning “this scrolls” and becomes just another style. The gap between the 22px panel radius and 9999px is what carries the signal, and it is wider now than it was.

The selected pill's day name is full white, not 80% white — the second recomputed departure from DESIGN.md. White at 80% over blue-600 composites to rgb(211,224,251) and measures 3.90:1, which fails AA for 12px text. Full white is 5.17:1; the date stays dominant because it is larger and set in the display face, not because the day name is faded.

07

Buttons & Inputs

Buttons are 56px tall, inputs 48px, both comfortably above the 44px tap minimum. Hover the primary to see the wipe travel along the axis, and tab through this plate to check every focus state.

primary · secondary · disabled — all 56px, rounded 12px

Nomor tidak valid. Gunakan format 08xx atau 62xx.

The wipe is the axis made interactive, and it is the only place a skewX is animated rather than static. It runs on transform alone, on a compositor layer, as a pseudo-element under the label so the text never moves relative to its own box — and it collapses to a plain colour change under prefers-reduced-motion.

The error state does not depend on its border alone. A border carrying a state by itself must clear 3:1 (WCAG 1.4.11). red-300 on a white field is 1.90:1 and fails, so the boundary is red-800 at 8.31:1 and the field fills red-100 — two signals, neither hue-only. Errors tie to their field via aria-describedby; focus rings are restyled, never removed.

The focus ring went from 2px/2px to 3px/3px with the redesign. At a 22px panel radius and 12px controls, a 2px ring at 2px offset gets visually absorbed by the corner; 3px clears it. It is the same ring on every focusable thing on the page — one ring, one offset, everywhere — with blue-400 substituted inside a navy band.

Elevation — three shadows and one glow, none inside a band

Depth comes from tonal layering — white plates on the blue ground, navy bands between them — not from shadows. Shadows exist to lift the two surfaces that genuinely float. Black shadows on a blue-white page read as dirt.

--shadow-sm

0 1px 2px · resting cards

--shadow-md

0 4px 12px · raised or hovered

--shadow-lg

0 24px 70px -30px · the order panel, once per page

--glow-interactive

0 10px 30px -12px blue · hovered available cells only

Why a third shadow, when two was the whole vocabulary. The order panel is now a single object holding the entire product — date row, slot grid, hand-off bar — sitting on the light ground beside a column of copy. shadow-md is edge definition; it does not read as an object at 660px wide. shadow-lg is a wide, very soft, heavily negative-spread navy shadow, which is depth rather than a border. The No-Shadow-On-Band Rule holds against all four: a shadow on a dark surface is either invisible or a glow, and glow is the anti-reference. Depth inside a band comes from navy-700 hairlines and the row hover tint.

Minimal in form, rich in behaviour. The client asked for a minimalist UI with many animations; read as one request rather than two, that is few elements each responding precisely. The restraint is what makes dense motion legible. One thing is latency rather than taste: when a slot is tapped the fill lands at 0ms and the decorative ring runs after, because the fill is the answer to “is 8pm mine now”. A single 300ms transition would have made that answer arrive 300ms late, which is a slower reply dressed as polish.

In production every effect routes through src/lib/motion.ts: GSAP has no built-in reduced-motion handling, so a direct gsap.to() in a component is banned. The Off-Screen-Is-Off Rule binds the only two continuously running animations in the system — the hero field and the marquee — which must stop via IntersectionObserver when their section leaves the viewport, under prefers-reduced-motion, and under saveData. Two uncapped requestAnimationFrame loops on a mid-range Android is the likeliest way this page fails a Lighthouse gate it would otherwise pass. Which effects the product uses is the user's choice, asked before code is written — see docs/design-process.md.
